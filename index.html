How to Prevent App Revokes on iOS (Complete Guide)
1
Install Anti-Revoke DNS: Tap Install on the GPL Mods Anti-Revoke Ultra profile in Safari. When prompted, tap Allow. Go to iOS Settings > Profile Downloaded > Install.
2
Enable DNS in iOS Settings: Confirm the DNS profile is selected in Settings > General > VPN & Device Management > DNS (set to GPL Mods Anti-Revoke).
3
Install Signed App: Select any signed app installer below (e.g. ESign, Scarlet, TrollStore) and tap Get to install directly onto your home screen via OTA.
4
Trust Enterprise Developer: Go to iOS Settings > General > VPN & Device Management, select the Enterprise Certificate, and tap Trust. On iOS 16+, make sure Developer Mode is on in Settings > Privacy & Security.
