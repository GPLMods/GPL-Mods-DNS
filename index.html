Sideloading 101: How It Works
What is Sideloading?
Sideloading is the process of installing application packages (.ipa files) onto an iPhone or iPad from sources outside Apple's official App Store.
  • 100% No Jailbreak: Operates strictly within iOS userland.
  • Warranty Safe: Does not modify bootrom or system kernel.
  • Empowers You: Run emulators, game mods, ad-free streaming, and custom dev utilities.
Code Signing Methods
Apple requires every app binary to carry a cryptographic digital signature:
  • Free Apple ID: Signs up to 3 apps valid for 7 days (AltStore, SideStore).
  • Enterprise Certificate: In-house corporate cert with unlimited apps and direct Safari OTA installation.
  • CoreTrust Bug: Permanent signing with zero revokes forever (TrollStore on iOS 14 - 17.0).
Anti-Revoke DNS Explained
When launching an enterprise-signed app, iOS phones home to Apple OCSP servers (ocsp.apple.com).
  • The Revoke Trigger: If Apple flagged the cert, iOS terminates the app with "Unable to Verify".
  • DNS Shield: Our encrypted DoH profile sinks Apple's verification queries to 0.0.0.0.
  • Result: iOS never receives the revocation signal, so apps open indefinitely!
Sandboxing & Security
Every sideloaded application runs inside iOS's fortified sandbox container:
  • Isolated Storage: Apps cannot peek into your banking data, personal photos, or system keychain.
  • Permission Prompts: Cameras, microphones, and location still require explicit iOS permission popups.
  • Easy Removal: Deleting an app completely removes all its data with zero system trace.

Common Fixes & Error Troubleshooting
"Unable to Verify App - Internet Connection Required"
Most Common Error
Why it happens: Apple's OCSP server revoked the enterprise certificate, OR your device contacted Apple while Anti-Revoke DNS was temporarily disabled or bypassed by another VPN.
  1. Do NOT delete the app yet if you have important data saved inside.
  2. Confirm that GPL Mods Anti-Revoke DNS is active in Settings > General > VPN & Device Management > DNS.
  3. Go to Settings > Safari and tap Clear History and Website Data. This purges cached OCSP revocation certificates.
  4. Toggle Airplane Mode ON for 10 seconds, then toggle it OFF.
  5. If the app still fails to verify, your device UDID is blacklisted for that specific certificate. Backup your data via iTunes / Files, and re-install the app signed with an alternate active certificate from the Store Archive.
"Developer Mode Required" (iOS 16, 17, 18+)
iOS 16+ Required
Why it happens: Apple introduced Developer Mode in iOS 16 to restrict running custom signed binaries without explicit user consent.
  1. Open the native iOS Settings app.
  2. Scroll down and tap Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll to the very bottom and tap Developer Mode.
  4. Toggle the switch to ON.
  5. A prompt will appear requesting a restart. Tap Restart.
  6. After your iPhone reboots and you unlock with your passcode, tap Turn On on the system prompt.
"Untrusted Enterprise Developer"
First-Time Install
Why it happens: iOS requires one-time manual verification before trusting an Enterprise Certificate profile.
  1. Open iOS Settings.
  2. Navigate to General > VPN & Device Management.
  3. Under the ENTERPRISE APP header, tap on the certificate name.
  4. Tap Trust "[Certificate Name]".
  5. Confirm by tapping Trust on the confirmation modal. You can now launch the app freely!
"App Integrity Could Not Be Verified" / Device Blacklisted
UDID Blacklist
Why it happens: Your device's unique hardware ID was logged by Apple's servers as using that specific revoked certificate before Anti-Revoke DNS was active.
  1. Clear Safari data: Settings > Safari > Clear History and Website Data.
  2. Delete the blocked application.
  3. Ensure GPL Mods Anti-Revoke DNS is installed and set to active.
  4. Re-install the application using a different Enterprise Certificate (GPL Mods maintains multiple independent certificate mirrors in the Store).
  5. Alternatively, install via SideStore or TrollStore to avoid enterprise blacklisting completely.
Anti-Revoke DNS Leaking / Not Blocking
VPN Conflict
Why it happens: A commercial VPN (like 1.1.1.1, Proton, or NordVPN) or iCloud Private Relay is overriding the encrypted DNS profile.
  1. Open Settings > [Your Apple ID Name] > iCloud > Private Relay and turn it OFF.
  2. Disconnect any third-party VPN apps (iOS routes traffic through active VPN tunnels over DNS profiles).
  3. Confirm profile selection in Settings > General > VPN & Device Management > DNS (check mark must be on GPL Mods Anti-Revoke Ultra).
  4. Verification Test: Open Safari and go to ocsp.apple.com. If Safari says "Cannot Open the Page" or "Server Cannot Be Found", your anti-revoke protection is 100% active and working!
App Crashes Immediately Upon Opening
Entitlement / JIT
Why it happens: The IPA was injected with an incompatible dylib, requires Just-In-Time (JIT) compilation, or was signed with a corrupt mobileprovision file.
  1. If the app is a heavy emulator (like Dolphin, PojavLauncher, or Flycast), it requires JIT compilation. Enable JIT via AltStore, SideStore, or TrollStore.
  2. Re-sign the IPA inside ESign with clean signing options: enable Remove get-task-allow and ensure Allow Execution is checked.
  3. Ensure the IPA version is compatible with your iOS version (e.g. 64-bit arm64 binary).
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
Can sideloading void my Apple warranty or brick my iPhone?

No, absolutely not. Sideloading does NOT modify your iOS bootloader, kernel, or internal firmware. It uses Apple's official code signing infrastructure.

Every sideloaded app is strictly sandboxed. You can delete any sideloaded application at any time just like a normal App Store app. Your iPhone hardware and warranty remain 100% intact.

Do I need a Mac or PC computer to sideload?

No computer is required when using Enterprise Signed Apps paired with Anti-Revoke DNS, or when using TrollStore on supported iOS versions.

You can install tools like ESign and Scarlet directly through Safari with a single tap. A computer is only needed if you choose tethered methods like AltStore or initial SideStore pairing.

How long do Enterprise certificates last?

Enterprise certificates are technically valid for up to 3 years. However, once Apple flags an enterprise certificate as publicly shared, Apple attempts to revoke it.

With GPL Mods Anti-Revoke DNS enabled on your device, Apple's revocation signal is completely blocked. Many users keep their enterprise signed apps working for months or even years without interruption!

What is the 3-app limit, and how can I bypass it?

Apple restricts personal, free Apple Developer accounts to installing a maximum of 3 sideloaded applications per device.

You can easily bypass this limitation by: (1) Using Enterprise Certificates (no app limits), (2) Using LiveContainer to run multiple apps inside one container, or (3) Using TrollStore if your iOS version is 14.0 – 17.0.

Does Anti-Revoke DNS slow down my internet or drain battery?

Zero speed loss and zero battery drain. The DNS profile uses native Apple iOS DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH) engine backed by Cloudflare and Google upstream servers.

It specifically and only filters Apple's OCSP revocation hostnames. All your regular web browsing, YouTube streaming, gaming, and app downloads run at maximum unthrottled line speed.

Can Apple ban my Apple ID for sideloading?

No. Sideloading is an official iOS developer feature used by millions of programmers and beta testers worldwide. For enterprise signed apps, you do not even enter your Apple ID credentials.

How do I update an app without losing my saved data?

When signing the updated IPA, ensure you use the exact same Bundle Identifier (e.g. com.spotify.client or com.google.ios.youtube).

iOS will identify the matching bundle ID and perform an in-place upgrade, preserving all your logins, local documents, and game save files!

What makes TrollStore different from normal sideloading?

TrollStore exploits the "CoreTrust" vulnerability discovered by security researchers on iOS 14.0 – 17.0. It tricks iOS into believing that any installed app is a factory-installed system binary.

Because of this, apps installed via TrollStore never expire, never revoke, have no 3-app limits, and can access special root entitlements like JIT compilation without requiring a jailbreak.
